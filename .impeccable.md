## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, design leaders, and collaborators evaluating Mayank's product-design judgment, systems thinking, and ability to build. The portfolio should make work easy to scan while rewarding deeper exploration with memorable, relevant interactions.

### Brand Personality
Personal, tactile, and confident. The experience should feel authored by a designer-builder: precise enough to inspire trust, human enough to invite conversation, and playful only when the interaction reinforces the story.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark, editorial portfolio with architectural spacing, restrained graphite surfaces, and physical paper moments used as deliberate contrast. The hiring letter should feel like a real object emerging from an envelope, not a novelty greeting card. Avoid generic glass effects, excessive decoration, bounce, and ornamental motion.

### Design Principles
- Let typography and negative space carry the interface; add visual effects only when they clarify hierarchy or state.
- Use one memorable interaction at a time, with motion that explains where an object came from and where it returns.
- Keep feedback immediate and exits faster than entrances; animate transform and opacity for smooth performance.
- Preserve keyboard access, clear focus states, and a non-spatial reduced-motion alternative.
- Keep portfolio content quickly scannable for hiring decisions while offering crafted details to people who explore.
